Nutrition data sources have separate terms and attribution:
USDA FoodData Central: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
Open Food Facts: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/

Newleaf journal data stays on your device unless you explicitly save/share an export.
Open Food Facts barcode lookup is free and requires no account. USDA search and
Describe suggestions use a free personal USDA API key; signup is required, but
hosting is not. The key is protected on the phone with Android Keystore and
excluded from exports and backups. An optional gateway can be used when the
personal key is blank; shared provider keys stay on that gateway.
No subscription or paid nutrition service is used.
Online Search and Describe food queries go directly to USDA with the personal
key, or through the optional gateway when no personal key is set. Barcode
lookup sends only the code to Open Food Facts. Suggested matches and inferred
portions require review; missing nutrition stays unknown. The gateway receives
no body weight, feelings, photos, or diary notes.
